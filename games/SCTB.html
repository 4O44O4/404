    Supercars Royale is the survival-racing game, full of speed, skill, and strategy in the high-stakes arena! Race massive super cars on floating bases comprised of weak hexagons that disappear beneath your tires one slip and you will fall into deep water.

Be the smartest, and take players down off the stages with perfect precision and bold moves. Win coins and unlock cars, upgrades and skins which will improve your performance and style.
